FEATURES

QUALIFYING

Set a hot lap on an empty track. Your time determines your starting grid position for the race.

Race from inside the car with a detailed cockpit featuring halo, steering wheel with speed and gear display, and mirrors.

Choose between Soft, Medium, and Hard tyre compounds. Manage tyre wear and fuel over 3 laps. Pit when you need to.

BOOST SYSTEM

Draft behind rivals to charge your boost meter. Deploy it anywhere on track for a burst of speed to overtake.
