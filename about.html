ABOUT THE GAME

Everything you need to know about Retro Grand Prix.

WHAT IS RETRO GRAND PRIX?

Retro Grand Prix is a pseudo-3D Formula 1 racing game built entirely with HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript. Inspired by classic arcade racers like Pole Position and OutRun, it combines retro visuals with modern F1 mechanics.

The game runs in any browser with no installation required. Just open and race.

THE CIRCUIT

Yas Marina Circuit, Abu Dhabi

The game features a faithful recreation of the Yas Marina Circuit with all 16 turns mapped to match the real track layout:

  • 1km pit straight into the heavy braking T1
  • T6 hairpin - the slowest point on the circuit
  • T8-9 sweeping left under the iconic W Hotel
  • 1.2km back straight - the DRS/boost zone
  • T10-11 fast S-curves (2021 redesigned section)
  • Marina section with yachts and waterfront towers
  • Full floodlighting for night racing

GAME MODES

QUALIFYING

One hot lap on an empty track. Your lap time is compared against AI-generated times for all 20 drivers. Your position determines your starting grid slot.

RACE

3 laps against 19 AI opponents. Manage tyres, fuel, and boost. Pit when needed. Fight for every position on the leaderboard.

KEY FEATURES

COCKPIT VIEW

Race from inside the car with halo, steering wheel displaying speed, gear, and DRS status, plus side mirrors.

TYRE STRATEGY

Three compounds - Soft (fast, wears quick), Medium (balanced), Hard (slow, lasts long). Dead tyres = massive grip loss.

BOOST SYSTEM

Slipstream behind rivals to charge your boost meter. Deploy it anywhere for +30 km/h top speed. Inspired by The Crew Motorfest.

FUEL MANAGEMENT

Fuel depletes over the race. Run out and your car coasts to a stop. Pit stops refuel to full automatically.

TECH STACK

Built with vanilla HTML5, CSS, and JavaScript. No frameworks, no build tools, no dependencies.

  • HTML5 Canvas for all rendering
  • Web Audio API for engine sounds and effects
  • Touch events for mobile support
  • Glassmorphism UI with backdrop-filter
  • Single-file game engine (~2000 lines)
