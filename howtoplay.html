HOW TO PLAY

Master the controls and strategy to win the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix.

CONTROLS

KEYBOARD

UP Accelerate
DOWN Brake
LEFT RIGHT Steer
SPACE Activate Boost
P Change pit tyre selection

MOBILE / TOUCH

On-screen buttons appear automatically on touch devices:

  • GAS - Accelerate (right side)
  • BRK - Brake (right side)
  • < > - Steer left/right (left side)
  • DRS - Activate boost (center)

TYRE STRATEGY

Three compounds available, each with trade-offs:

SOFT Fastest grip, +10 km/h speed bonus. Wears out quickly.
MEDIUM Balanced performance and durability. The safe choice.
HARD Slowest but lasts longest. -5 km/h but barely wears.

Warning: At 100% tyre wear, you lose 50% grip and 60% max speed. Pit before that happens!

BOOST SYSTEM

Inspired by The Crew Motorfest. Your boost meter charges and depletes:

  • Slipstream charging - Tuck behind an AI car to charge fast. "SLIPSTREAM - CHARGING" appears in blue.
  • Passive charging - Slow trickle charge while driving above 50 km/h.
  • Activation - Press SPACE when you have charge. +30 km/h top speed.
  • Depletion - Boost drains while held. Release to save charge for later.

The boost bar above the cockpit shows your charge level. The steering wheel's right circle fills as a pie chart.

PIT STOPS

Near the end of the lap (approaching the pit straight), a hint appears:

"PIT LANE: Steer LEFT to enter"

  • Steer left (past x = -0.7) to enter the pit lane
  • The car slows to pit speed limit automatically
  • Tyres are changed and fuel is refilled to full
  • You rejoin the race - AI cars keep racing during your stop!
  • Press P before entering to choose your tyre compound

FUEL

Your car starts with a full tank. Fuel depletes over the race.

  • Full fuel adds weight, reducing top speed slightly
  • As fuel burns off, the car gets lighter and faster
  • At 0% fuel, the car coasts to a stop - no acceleration possible
  • Pit stops refuel to full automatically

TIPS

  • Start on Soft tyres for a fast qualifying lap
  • In the race, consider starting on Mediums to delay your pit stop
  • Slipstream behind cars on straights to charge boost, then use it to overtake
  • Don't hit the guardrails - you lose 15% speed on impact
  • Watch the mini track map to see where rivals are
  • Pit before your tyres hit 80% wear for the best strategy
